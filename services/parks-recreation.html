Parks and recreation

Park hours, pavilion and athletic field reservations, the Sablewood Aquatic Center, and rules that apply in every city park.

Park hours

City parks are open from 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. daily unless posted otherwise. Sablewood Nature Preserve closes at sunset year-round.

Reserving a pavilion

Pavilion reservations open 90 days in advance and close 72 hours before the date. Reserve online or at the Parks office.

The deposit is refunded within 10 business days if the pavilion is left clean and undamaged. An unreserved pavilion is first-come, first-served; a printed confirmation is what settles a dispute on the day.

Athletic fields

Fields at the Sports Complex are allocated to affiliated youth leagues first, and open for public reservation on any date not allocated by March 1 for spring or August 1 for fall. Field lights run until 10:00 p.m. and are billed at $22 per hour.

Aquatic Center

The Sablewood Aquatic Center opens the Saturday of Memorial Day weekend and closes the day after Labor Day. Daily admission is $4 for residents and $7 for non-residents; children two and under are admitted free. A resident season pass is $85 for an individual and $210 for a household of up to five.

Park rules

  • Dogs must be leashed. The only off-leash area is the fenced run at Heritage Park.
  • Glass containers are prohibited in all parks.
  • Alcohol is prohibited except by permit at Founders Green during a permitted event.
  • Motorized vehicles are restricted to paved parking areas. Trails are for foot and non-motorized traffic only.
  • Amplified sound requires a permit and must end by 9:00 p.m.
  • Charcoal grills are allowed only in the fixed grills provided. Open ground fires are prohibited year-round.
